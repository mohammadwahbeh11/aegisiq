Questions people actually ask

Is Community really free forever?

Yes. It is Elastic License 2.0 — you can self-host, modify, and use it in production, including in commercial deployments. The only thing ELv2 restricts is offering AegisIQ as a competing hosted product to other people.

What runs where?

Community runs 100% on your own hardware — a $20/month VPS is fine for a 50-person org. Pro adds cloud-hosted AI + threat-intel enrichment; you can also run entirely self-hosted by pointing Copilot at your own Ollama instance and skipping enrichment.

What data leaves my network on Pro?

On Pro, alert summaries (redacted of raw log bodies) are sent to your chosen AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, or your own Ollama). IP addresses you look up hit AbuseIPDB and OTX. Compliance reports never leave your instance. Everything is documented in docs/DATA_FLOW.md.

Do you support Arabic?

Yes — the AI Copilot answers in Arabic when you set lang=ar, playbooks are being translated, and the UI ships RTL-ready components. This is a first-class feature, not an afterthought.

Cancel anytime?

Yes. Pro is month-to-month via Stripe. Cancel from the billing portal; you keep access to the end of the current period. Downgrading to Community keeps all your data and rules — the Pro features simply gate off.
