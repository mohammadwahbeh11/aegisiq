Problem

Enterprise SIEM is priced for the Fortune 500. MENA SMBs are locked out.

  • Splunk starts at $1,800/GB/year — a 50-employee retailer paying $60k+/year before a single analyst is hired.
  • English-only tooling. Alerts, runbooks, and dashboards force Arabic-speaking analysts to context-switch on every triage.
  • Compliance evidence is a manual month of work. SOC 2 / ISO 27001 / GDPR auditors show up quarterly; nobody has a live report to hand them.
  • No local threat intel context. Global feeds miss regional actor infrastructure and Arabic-language phishing lures.

Solution

Open-core SIEM/SOAR with bilingual AI Copilot, live compliance evidence, and integrated threat intel.

  • Self-hosted or managed. Community tier free forever (Elastic License 2.0).
  • AI Copilot explains every alert in Arabic or English with recommended actions.
  • Composite IP reputation (AbuseIPDB × OTX) built into every alert.
  • Live SOC 2 / ISO 27001 / GDPR evidence reports from the audit log.
  • MITRE ATT&CK & Cyber Kill Chain mapping on every detection rule.
  • Kill-Switch agent — HMAC-signed containment orders to endpoints in <3 s.
  • MFA (TOTP RFC 6238), AES-256-GCM secret encryption, 8-layer defense-in-depth.
  • Pluggable event store (SQLite / OpenSearch / ClickHouse).
  • Sigma rule import — the industry's detection lingua franca.
  • Runs on a $20/month VPS. Analyst gets value on day one.
